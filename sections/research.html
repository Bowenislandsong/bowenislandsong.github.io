Research

Deployable ML for sensing, personalization, and systems constraints.

My research asks how models stay useful when the data are noisy, users differ, privacy matters, latency matters, and evaluation has to survive contact with real systems.

My active research is personalized AI for health, especially wearable sensing and biomechanical estimation. Across projects, I prioritize efficient models and systems-aware evaluation.

Research Themes

Three research themes.

Embodied ML that stays meaningful on device

Wearable and motion data are useful only when the model respects physics, sensor placement, and limited deployment budgets.

Personalization with privacy built in

Average-case performance is often the wrong target. I care about adaptation across users and devices without assuming private data can be pooled freely.

Research systems that scale in reality

The infrastructure around a model determines whether experiments are reproducible, evaluations are trustworthy, and results can be extended.

IEEE TBME Health AI 2024

Estimating Ground Reaction Forces From Inertial Sensors

B. Song, M. Paolieri, H. E. Stewart, L. Golubchik, J. L. McNitt-Gray, V. Misra, D. Shah

Introduces SVD Embedding Regression, a low-rank alternative to heavy sequence models for latency-sensitive wearable sensing. The main point is practical: a smaller, clearer model can match or beat heavier LSTM baselines.

IEEE CLOUD Cloud systems 2022

Performance and Revenue Analysis of Hybrid Cloud Federations with QoS Requirements

Bowen Song, Marco Paolieri, Leana Golubchik

A queueing-theoretic and incentive-aware framework for deciding when federated hybrid clouds improve QoS, cost, and fairness.

Allerton Distributed data 2019

Scalable String Reconciliation by Recursive Content-Dependent Shingling (RCDS)

Bowen Song, Ari Trachtenberg

A communication-efficient reconciliation protocol for large, incrementally changing data, evaluated on real revision histories and compared against rsync.
