Research Agenda

Three themes behind the publication list.

Embodied ML that stays meaningful on device

Build interpretable, physically meaningful models that stay efficient enough for edge devices and robust enough for messy sensing.

Wearable and motion data are a demanding testbed because the model has to respect physics, stay legible, and still run efficiently when the person, sensor placement, and environment all change at once.

Personalization with privacy built in

Adapt to users, devices, and settings without brittle retraining or privacy assumptions that break in real deployment.

I keep returning to lightweight adaptation, few-shot personalization, and federated learning because average-case performance is often the wrong target, and private user data rarely lives in one clean training loop.

Research systems that scale in reality

Build scalable infrastructure and evaluation so research stays reliable as data, workloads, and deployment demands grow.

This is the through-line from cloud theory to ranking to OpenShift: scale is the main systems question, and reliability only matters if the model and surrounding pipeline still hold up as the problem gets bigger.

IEEE TBME Health AI 2024

Estimating Ground Reaction Forces From Inertial Sensors

B. Song, M. Paolieri, H. E. Stewart, L. Golubchik, J. L. McNitt-Gray, V. Misra, D. Shah

Introduces SVD Embedding Regression, a low-rank alternative to heavy sequence models for latency-sensitive wearable sensing.

Signal: I challenge the assumption that a bigger sequence model is automatically better.

PDF IEEE Xplore

This paper matters beyond biomechanics because it shows a habit I trust: when deployment is constrained, a smaller and clearer model can be more valuable than a heavier one with marginal gains.

We study whether inertial measurement units collected during steady-state running can estimate ground reaction forces and derived biomechanical variables with lightweight models. The paper proposes SVD Embedding Regression and shows that low-rank methods can match or beat LSTM baselines while remaining practical for edge deployment and personalization.

IEEE CLOUD Cloud systems 2022

Performance and Revenue Analysis of Hybrid Cloud Federations with QoS Requirements

Bowen Song, Marco Paolieri, Leana Golubchik

A queueing-theoretic and incentive-aware framework for when federated hybrid clouds improve QoS, cost, and fairness.

Signal: I can combine formal modeling, incentives, and systems thinking in one contribution.

PDF IEEE Xplore

Queueing analysis, QoS, and revenue sharing are all treated as one federation problem instead of separate side issues.

This paper analyzes hybrid cloud federations where members share local resources and distribute savings according to Shapley value. It characterizes when full sharing is optimal, when heterogeneous QoS makes the problem harder, and how to discourage free riders without central trust.

Allerton Distributed data 2019

Scalable String Reconciliation by Recursive Content-Dependent Shingling (RCDS)

Bowen Song, Ari Trachtenberg

A communication-efficient reconciliation protocol for large, incrementally changing data, tested against real revision histories.

Signal: I can take an algorithmic idea all the way to real-workload evidence against a standard tool.

PDF IEEE Xplore

The algorithm is paired with real revision-history benchmarks against rsync, not just theory.

RCDS addresses the remote reconciliation of similar strings with low communication cost. It scales linearly with edit distance, is practical on large strings, and outperforms rsync on a substantial fraction of realistic workloads.

Incentives + systems

Shapley Value Calculator

Reusable payoff allocation package for federation, resource sharing, and incentives.

Large-scale reconciliation

RCDS_GO

Go implementation of remote large-string reconciliation for synchronizing huge files by transferring differences instead of whole payloads.

Efficient reconciliation is useful anywhere synchronization is expensive, from cloud hosts to DNA or RNA sequences, and potentially to future model-state transfer for very large weights or KV-cache-like state.
