Systems Strengths

What I emphasize on the engineering side.

Platform ownership

I am comfortable owning the release, dependency, and interoperability problems that make platforms hard to keep stable.

The OpenShift work is the cleanest proof here: complex operator packaging, upgrade safety, and cross-team integration all had to be right at the same time.

Reliable data movement

Streaming and analytics systems only help if they stay correct through upgrade paths, failures, and schema drift.

The eBay infrastructure internship was especially useful because exactly-once semantics, ClickHouse ingestion, and multi-datacenter realities made correctness a first-order concern.

Evaluation and operability

I do not separate model quality from the infrastructure and measurement quality around the model.

That carries from flaky-test detection to ranking evaluation to research pipelines: if the surrounding loop is weak, the result is weak too.
