Approach

How I work across research and engineering.

Prefer simpler methods when they win

I value clear metrics, strong baselines, and efficient, interpretable methods. In my SER work, low-rank embeddings and linear maps matched or exceeded LSTM accuracy for GRF estimation while remaining far cheaper to train and run on edge devices.

Same rigor across wearable ML and systems

In both wearable ML and cloud systems, I care about careful evaluation and methods that work under practical constraints.

Build the system around the model

My ranking, streaming, and Kubernetes work has taught me to consider scale, reliability, and observability alongside model quality.

Teaching and mentorship

I mentored students in biomechanics and systems and was a networking TA at USC. Clear feedback, reproducible experiments, and careful course operations make teams and classrooms stronger.
