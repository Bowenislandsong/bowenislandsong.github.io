Approach

How I work across research and engineering.

Prefer simpler methods when they win

Clear metrics, strong baselines, and methods that stay interpretable and efficient under deployment constraints. SER is the clearest example: low-rank embeddings and linear maps matched or exceeded LSTM accuracy for GRF estimation while remaining far cheaper to train and run on edge devices.

Same rigor across wearable ML and systems

From IMU-based biomechanics to cloud federation models, the goal is solutions that hold up under real constraints: reason under uncertainty, evaluate honestly, and ship systems others can trust.

Build the system around the model

Ranking, streaming, and Kubernetes experience keep scale, reliability, and observability in scope from day one. Infrastructure is part of ML impact: results matter when teams can run, debug, and extend them.

Teaching and mentorship

Mentored students across biomechanics and systems; TA experience in networking at USC. Clear feedback, reproducible experiments, and careful course operations make teams and classrooms stronger.
