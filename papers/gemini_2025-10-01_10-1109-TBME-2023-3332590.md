abstract: Accurate ground reaction force (GRF) estimation is crucial for gait analysis and rehabilitation. While deep learning models using inertial measurement units (IMUs) have shown promise, the need for large, labeled datasets from diverse individuals remains a challenge. This paper proposes a federated learning (FL) framework for personalized GRF estimation, enabling collaborative model training across multiple clients (subjects) without sharing raw data. Each client trains a local model using their own IMU and GRF data, and then the model updates are aggregated on a central server. A personalization layer is added to each client’s local model to capture subject-specific characteristics and improve individual GRF estimation accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed FL framework, achieving comparable or superior performance to centralized training while preserving data privacy.

## Summary
This paper presents a federated learning (FL) approach to personalize ground reaction force (GRF) estimation using inertial measurement units (IMUs). The FL framework enables collaborative training of deep learning models across multiple subjects without the need to share raw data, addressing privacy concerns. A personalized layer is incorporated into each local model to capture subject-specific characteristics and enhance estimation accuracy.

## Key Contributions and Insights
*   A novel federated learning framework for personalized GRF estimation using IMU data, ensuring data privacy.
*   Integration of a personalization layer into each local model to capture subject-specific characteristics and improve GRF estimation accuracy.
*   Demonstration of comparable or superior performance compared to centralized training methods.
*   Reduced reliance on large, centralized datasets, fostering collaborative research while safeguarding sensitive data.

## Why this is State-of-the-Art
*   Addresses the critical challenge of data privacy in GRF estimation research by using federated learning.
*   Leverages a personalized layer within the FL framework, tailoring the model to individual gait patterns for enhanced accuracy.
*   Demonstrates the feasibility and effectiveness of FL for GRF estimation, promoting collaborative research across diverse datasets and institutions.

## Weaknesses or Limitations and How to Improve
*   The personalization layer's architecture and optimization process could be further refined. Explore more advanced personalization techniques.
*   The robustness of the FL framework to heterogeneous datasets (e.g., varying IMU sensor placements, gait speeds) needs further evaluation. Test using more varied datasets and develop domain adaptation techniques within the FL framework.
*   Communication costs and model aggregation strategies within the FL framework need to be optimized for real-time applications. Investigate compression techniques and efficient aggregation algorithms.
